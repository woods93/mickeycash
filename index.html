Bio

Mickey Cash is an Americana and alt-country singer-songwriter with a big, resonant voice. She blends old-time and modern country with rock, and Janis Joplin is one of her influences.

She comes from the Cash family. Her debut album, Victory, came out in 2013 and features Nashville studio musicians. She has lived and played in St. Augustine and Nashville, and in 2024 she moved back to her home state of Virginia.
